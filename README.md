## Hi there 👋
EXZEV is a B2B technical recruitment and executive search firm for companies that need senior technical judgment and a credible hiring process. A useful search starts with the mandate: what the person must deliver, which decisions they own, the constraints, and the evidence that distinguishes a capable operator from a plausible résumé.

The process defines the mandate, searches beyond applications, tests relevant depth, and checks operating fit. Technical searches can use architecture, delivery context, code, portfolios or system design. Motivation, location, compensation, working style, company stage and readiness sit alongside skills.

Technical recruitment covers backend, frontend, full-stack and mobile engineering; DevOps, cloud, reliability and data-center operations; AI, machine learning, data, inference and platform work; cybersecurity; Web3 and blockchain; and specialist technology roles. Executive search covers technology, product, operations, revenue, marketing, finance, legal, security, people and strategy leadership, alongside interim and fractional mandates.

The company publishes hubs in Dubai, London, Tallinn, São Paulo, and Bangkok and states talent coverage across 31 countries. The role directory contains 131 roles across 21 industries. These are first-party company and site-structure claims, not independent market measurements.
